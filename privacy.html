Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy explains what the ASE Test Prep by MotiveU app ("MotiveU", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the iPhone and Android apps.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account details: name, email address, password (stored hashed by our database provider), role (technician or manager), shop and experience level. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email Apple shares with us.To create and secure your account, and to connect technicians with their shop.
Study activity: test answers and scores, mastery by category, diagnostic case attempts, XP, streaks, achievements, challenges, contest entries and study plan settings.To run the app, show your progress, rank challenges, and let your shop manager see results when you belong to a shop.
Diagnostic write-ups you choose to submit.Sent to an AI service provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to grade them. They are not used to identify you.
Purchase records: the App Store or Google Play transaction ID, product and expiry date. We never see your card details.To verify subscriptions with Apple or Google and unlock MotiveU Pro.
App usage events, such as when a paywall is shown or a purchase starts.To understand and improve the app.
Device identifiers: a push notification token, and the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android).To send notifications you turned on, and to measure which of our ads led to an install, through the Meta (Facebook) SDK.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads in the app. We share data only with providers that run parts of the service for us:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Apple and Google, which process payments and confirm purchases.
  • Meta Platforms, which receives app events (install, open, registration, purchase) and the advertising identifier where allowed, to measure our advertising.
  • Expo, which delivers push notifications.
  • OpenAI or Anthropic, which grade write-ups you submit.
  • Your shop manager, if you join a shop, can see your test results and assignments.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • On iPhone you can decline tracking when asked, or later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time. See Delete your account.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit. We keep your account data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account.

Children

MotiveU is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
